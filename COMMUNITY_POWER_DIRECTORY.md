# Community Power Directory: Paid Directory Business Model

## Purpose
The Community Power Directory is a curated, high-trust marketplace that connects people to vetted vendors, farms, co-ops, creators, and community resources. It is designed to save time, increase visibility for aligned businesses, and build community wealth through curated discovery and trust-first verification.

## Value Proposition
- **Core promise:** Save time, increase visibility, and strengthen community wealth through curated discovery.
- **Why people pay:** Curation, time saved, trust and verification, visibility/SEO benefits, and mission alignment.

## Revenue Model
### Paid Listings
- **Tiers:**
  - **Basic ($10/mo):** Standard listing with clickable website and Instagram plus map location.
  - **Featured ($25/mo):** Highlighted listing, search priority, and one spotlight post per quarter.
  - **Premium ($97/mo):** Top placement, custom listing design, SEO optimization, short promo reel, and a metrics dashboard.
- **Example monthly projection:** 350 Featured listings ($8,750) + 150 Premium listings ($14,550) = **$23,300**.

### Sponsored Placement
- Sponsored categories, seasonal guides, and spotlight articles at **$250–$1,500**.
- Use cases: Top 10 lists, holiday blocks, and vendor-of-the-month features.

### Affiliate & Marketplace Layer
- Ticket-sales percentage, workshop/event commissions, referral fees to partner platforms, or integrated merch/digital shop revenue.

## Operations & Automation
- Self-service listings with automated form → payment → listing creation.
- Auto-renewing payments.
- AI agents for periodic scraping to refresh public info, monthly SEO updates, and data cleanup (consistency checks, broken link detection).

## Tech Stack Options
- **Cloudflare Workers + D1:** Low cost, fast, and compatible with JSON-first directory logic.
- **Webflow + Airtable:** No-code, quick launch, visually rich directories.
- **Astro or React static site:** SEO-friendly, simple JSON data, and hostable anywhere.

## Integration With Existing Ecosystem
- Bridge to **Action Network** for content pipelines and traffic amplification.
- **Solar Roots** for local vendor and farm connections.
- **Mutual Aid Mapping** to raise resource visibility.
- **Political Real Estate Maps** to layer community power data.
- **Substack traffic** to drive SEO via educational posts.

## 30-Day Launch Plan
- **Week 1:** Finalize categories, define schema, build the MVP directory.
- **Week 2:** Set up paid tiers, build onboarding form, create vendor pitch kit.
- **Week 3:** Outreach to first 50 vendors, launch Instagram promo content, soft launch to community.
- **Week 4:** Add sponsored blocks, enable search/filters, and begin monthly SEO automation.

## Listing Schema
Fields include: business_name, location, category, website, instagram, email, phone, description, pricing, hours, season, map_coordinates, tier, media, and tags.

## Freemium Flow
- **Free listing:** Name + category + city.
- **Upgrade upsell:** Visibility boosts, SEO support, custom design, and promo reel.
- **Conversion channels:** Email, Instagram DM, and landing page CTAs.
